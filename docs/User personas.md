## User Personas: Saarathi & Kairo

To ensure **Saarathi** and its AI companion **Kairo** solve real-world productivity hurdles, development is anchored around three distinct user personas representing primary target audiences.

---

### Persona 1: The Ambitious Computer Science Student

* **Name:** Rahul Varma
* **Age:** 20
* **Occupation:** B.Tech Information Technology Student & Competitive Programmer
* **Context:** Balancing intensive coursework, data structures and algorithms (DSA) practice, hackathons, and extracurricular student chapter commitments in Hyderabad.

#### Pain Points

* **Context Switching Fatigue:** Constantly jumps between college assignments, coding contest deadlines, and group project tasks, often losing track of priorities.
* **Procrastination under Pressure:** Puts off high-friction tasks (like difficult algorithm sets or documentation) until late at night, leading to erratic sleep schedules and burnout.
* **Scattered Information:** Stores ideas for startup projects and hackathons across random notes apps, WhatsApp chats, and browser tabs, making them impossible to retrieve later.

#### How Saarathi & Kairo Help

* **Brain Dump Mode:** Rahul can record a quick 2-minute voice note after class summarizing his messy to-do list, which Kairo instantly parses into structured, prioritized tasks.
* **Procrastination Predictor:** Kairo analyzes historical skip patterns and warns him: *"You usually skip DSA prep on Monday evenings when fatigued. Move this session to your peak window tomorrow morning?"*
* **Long-Term Memory:** Using Supabase Vector Search, Rahul can ask Kairo: *"What was that decentralized verification platform architecture we discussed last month?"* to retrieve deep project context instantly.

### Persona 2: The Full-Stack Developer / Freelancer

* **Name:** Priya Sharma
* **Age:** 24
* **Occupation:** Junior Software Engineer & Open-Source Contributor
* **Context:** Working remotely, managing multiple client repositories, side hustles, personal skill development roadmaps, and deep-focus coding blocks.

#### Pain Points

* **Attention Fragmentation:** Constant notifications and shifting priorities disrupt deep work states, making it difficult to log meaningful uninterrupted coding hours.
* **Energy Misalignment:** Schedules heavy architectural refactoring during low-energy post-lunch hours, resulting in slow progress and frustration.
* **Goal Breakdown Overwhelm:** Broad career goals (e.g., *"Become an AI Engineer"*) feel too vast, making it hard to know what concrete steps to take each day.

#### How Saarathi & Kairo Help

* **Energy Clustering & Smart Scheduler:** KMeans clustering identifies Priya's peak coding hours (9 AM–12 PM) and automatically blocks her calendar, protecting her deep work time from distractions.
* **Goal System:** Kairo breaks down her long-term career goal into quarterly milestones, weekly learning modules, and daily actionable coding tasks.
* **Focus Mode:** Minimalist UI with built-in Pomodoro timers and ambient soundscapes that keep her locked into tasks without switching apps.

---

### Persona 3: The Multi-Tasking Professional / Organizer

* **Name:** Ananya Reddy
* **Age:** 26
* **Occupation:** Product Coordinator & Community Event Organizer
* **Context:** Juggling daily operational meetings, sponsor communications, task delegations, volunteer coordination, and tight execution deadlines.

#### Pain Points

* **Information Overload:** Overwhelmed by massive streams of daily updates, meeting action items, and shifting project timelines.
* **Lack of Visibility:** Struggles to see macro-trends in productivity—such as which days of the week experience the highest task drop-off rates.
* **Reactive Planning:** Spends the first hour of every workday figuring out what to do instead of executing high-leverage work.

#### How Saarathi & Kairo Help

* **The Daily Brief:** Every morning, Kairo greets Ananya with a personalized voice/text briefing summarizing yesterday's completion metrics, today's schedule, and optimal focus windows.
* **Analytics Dashboard:** Provides a clear visual breakdown of completed tasks, habit heatmaps, and productivity scores using interactive charts.
* **Natural Language Planning:** Allows her to quickly type or speak complex schedules on the go via the mobile app, with instant synchronization to the Vite React web dashboard.
